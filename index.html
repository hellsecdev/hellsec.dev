How we work

What happens after you press Discuss project.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We map your goals, systems, workflows and constraints. You get focused questions, not a sales deck.

  2. 02

    Audit

    We review architecture, access and risk before anything is built. Scope and acceptance criteria are agreed in writing.

  3. 03

    Build

    We develop and test against the agreed scope. You see progress at agreed checkpoints.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We document the system, access and recovery steps. Code, accounts and knowledge stay with your business.

  5. 05

    Support

    We stay available for an agreed support period after launch. Further improvements are scoped only when you need them.
