AI Center for clinics and service businesses. Scope, timing and integrations are agreed after discovery.

Inquiries get lost in chats. The owner does not see the full queue.

WhatsApp, Instagram and reception each hold a piece of the deal. Follow-up depends on memory. Without a shared record, the price and next step can be forgotten.

What breaks today

  • Leads split across WhatsApp, Instagram and email
  • No clear owner or follow-up date
  • High-value inquiries wait while the team chases noise
  • Access and admin paths stay undocumented

What AI Center puts in place

  • One queue for leads, status and next step
  • AI summaries so the owner reads less and decides faster
  • Daily owner report with price-ready follow-ups
  • You get the map, the queue and the access list.
