Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to read about our services and contact us. This statement explains what we have done on hellsec.dev and how to reach us if something does not work for you.

Standard

The site aims to meet Israeli Standard SI 5568 at level AA, based on WCAG 2.0, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013. We also follow WCAG 2.1 AA where it applies.

What we have done

  • Pages use real headings, landmarks and lists, and every page has one main heading.
  • Each page declares its language. Hebrew pages are laid out right to left.
  • Text meets a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
  • The site works with a keyboard: a "Skip to content" link, visible focus, and keyboard-operable menus, FAQ items and forms.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are announced in text.
  • Animations are reduced when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages adapt to small screens and to zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.

Accessibility toolbar

Every page has an accessibility toolbar: the round purple button in the bottom left corner. It lets you:

  • enlarge text, switch to high contrast, dark mode or grayscale, adjust saturation and text spacing;
  • use a dyslexia-friendly font (Latin letters only), hide images, highlight links and show image descriptions;
  • turn on a reading guide, line focus, a large cursor and stronger focus indicators;
  • have text read aloud, stop animations and mute sounds.

Keyboard shortcuts include Alt+F for font size, Alt+C for contrast and Alt+D for dark mode. Your choices are saved only in your browser.

Known limitations

  • The dyslexia font covers Latin letters; Russian and Hebrew text keeps the regular font.
  • Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in voices, so the available languages depend on your device.
  • The dashboards in our product and scenario pages are illustrations made of text and numbers; if any part is unclear, contact us and we will explain it.

How we checked

Last checked on 4 October 2026. We ran automated checks on every page (contrast, page language and structure, layout at 360 px width, Lighthouse accessibility audits of key pages) and tested the navigation, menus, FAQ, forms and the accessibility toolbar with a keyboard. This is our own review, not an external certification.
