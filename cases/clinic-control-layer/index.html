Before: the owner had messages, not a system.

  • New inquiries arrived through WhatsApp, Instagram and phone notes.
  • Reception handled urgent messages first, while warm leads waited.
  • The owner could not see which opportunities needed a decision before the end of the day.
  • Access and handover notes were implicit, not documented.
02

HellSec layer: one operating view.

  • Single intake queue with source, status, owner and next action.
  • AI summary field for need, objection, value and recommended reply.
  • Daily owner brief with new leads, overdue follow-up and blocked decisions.
  • Security baseline for admin paths, vendor access and recovery notes.

FAQ

Is this a full CRM migration?

No. The first version can be a lightweight control layer before a larger CRM decision.

Can this work with WhatsApp?

Yes. The safe first step can be manual or semi-automated, then integrations can be added when the process is stable.

What makes it secure?

Access, admin paths, data sensitivity and recovery notes are documented as part of handover.
