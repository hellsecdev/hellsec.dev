Who it's for

AI Center is for owners who need the queue before more automation.

Best when leads, follow-up, access and handover already affect revenue or daily decisions.

Good fit

  • Leads arrive in chats, forms and notes.
  • Follow-up depends on memory or one person.
  • Access or handover is unclear.

Not the right first step

  • No repeatable sales or service process yet.
  • AI demos with no queue or owner.
  • No one will own the queue after launch.

FAQ

Questions about AI Center.

What is AI Center?

AI Center is HellSec's product that brings inquiry intake, CRM, follow-up reminders, AI summaries, reply templates and a daily owner report into one queue.

Do we need a complex CRM?

No. Many businesses should start with Google Sheets, Airtable or Notion and upgrade only after the process is clear.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. The first version can use a safe manual or semi-automated flow, then add direct integrations where appropriate.
