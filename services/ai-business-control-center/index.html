FAQ

What is ABCC?

ABCC is HellSec's AI Business Control Center: a practical system for lead intake, CRM, follow-up reminders, AI summaries, reply templates and daily owner reporting.

Do we need a complex CRM?

No. Many businesses should start with Google Sheets, Airtable or Notion and upgrade only after the process is clear.

Can it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. The first version can use a safe manual or semi-automated flow, then add direct integrations where appropriate.
