How we work

Principles we hold on every project.

  1. 01

    Security in the architecture

    Access, data paths and recovery are designed before code is written, not patched after launch.

  2. 02

    You own what we build

    Source code, environments, accounts and documentation live in your accounts from day one.

  3. 03

    Direct contact with seniors

    You talk to the people who design and build the system. No account managers in between.

  4. 04

    Scope agreed in writing

    Goals, acceptance criteria and timing are agreed before work starts. Changes are agreed the same way.

  5. 05

    Confidential by default

    We sign an NDA on request before you share details, and keep access to the minimum the work needs.

  6. 06

    Clear handover

    Every project ends with documented access, recovery steps and the next decisions for your team.
